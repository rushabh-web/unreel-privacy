Unreel — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 July 2026

Unreel is a screen-time management app. This policy explains what data the app handles and what never leaves your phone.

Data that stays on your device

Everything Unreel exists to do happens locally:

  • Which apps you track and the daily limits you set
  • Your screen-time history and statistics
  • Your chosen nudge video or audio file (Unreel stores only a reference to the file you picked; the file itself is never copied or uploaded)
  • All settings, including snooze budget and appearance

None of this is ever transmitted off your device. Unreel contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no trackers. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

Data we collect: your account

Unreel uses Google Firebase Authentication to sign you in. Depending on the sign-in method you choose, Firebase processes and stores:

  • Your email address (email or Google sign-in)
  • Your name, if you add one or sign in with Google

This information is used solely to operate your account. We do not share it with anyone, use it for advertising, or link it to your screen-time data (which never leaves the phone). Firebase's handling of this data is described in Google's Firebase privacy documentation.

Permissions the app requests

  • Usage access — to measure how long your chosen apps are on screen. Read locally, stored locally.
  • Display over other apps — to show your nudge at the moment your limit is reached. Always dismissible.
  • Notifications — for the monitoring status and the 80% heads-up.
  • Ignore battery optimizations (optional) — so your phone's battery saver doesn't silence your nudges.

Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your Unreel account and the email address associated with it. No waiting period, no support request needed.
  • Without the app: email rushabhpandit1018@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Unreel account". The account is deleted within 30 days.
  • On-device data (screen-time history, limits, settings): uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. This data is never uploaded, so uninstalling removes it permanently.

Deleting your account removes all account data we hold. Nothing is retained after deletion.

Children

Unreel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and material changes will be noted in the app's update notes.
